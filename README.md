## 🌱 Concept & Sustainability Theme
Plastic pollution threatens freshwater ecosystems across Southeast Asia. **EcoSort: River Cleanup** challenges players to pilot a cleanup vessel to intercept floating plastic waste before it flows downstream, while avoiding local aquatic wildlife.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Mechanics
* **HTML5 Canvas & CSS3:** Responsive 2D rendering and HUD overlay.
* **Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+):** Custom `requestAnimationFrame` game loop, AABB collision detection, dynamic difficulty scaling, and state management.

## 🕹️ How to Play
* **Move Left:** `A` or `Left Arrow`
* **Move Right:** `D` or `Right Arrow`
* **Objective:** Collect floating trash (`🧴`, `🥤`, `🛍️`) for **+10 points**. Avoid hitting river fish (`🐟`) or letting trash slip past your boat, which costs a life.
